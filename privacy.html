Welcome to Pitter-Patter Raindrops (the “App”). This app is created especially for young children, ages 0–5, and their families.

Your child’s privacy matters greatly to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App works and how we handle information.

1. We Do Not Collect Personal Information

Pitter-Patter Raindrops does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

We do not ask for or gather:

  • Names or birthdates
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Home addresses or location data
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Device identifiers or tracking IDs
  • Analytics connected to individual users

There are no accounts, no logins, and no sign-ups.

2. Safe for Young Children

This App is built to be enjoyed by babies, toddlers, and young children with supervision from a grown-up.

We follow children’s privacy laws, including:

  • COPPA (USA – Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • Applicable international child-privacy protections

Because we don’t collect any personal information, parental permission is not required.

3. How the App Works

Pitter-Patter Raindrops is a tap-to-read and tap-to-interact storybook.

  • All actions happen only on the device
  • No information leaves the device
  • No online features or messaging
  • No external links for children to tap

4. No Ads + No Trackers

The App does not use:

  • Advertising networks or ad services
  • Tracking or analytics tools that follow user behavior
  • Social media plugins
  • Third-party data collectors

If payment is required, it is handled securely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or access payment information.

5. No Sensitive Device Access

The App does not request access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Photo library

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will change the “Last Updated” date shown above.
