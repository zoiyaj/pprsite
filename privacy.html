Welcome to Pitter-Patter Raindrops (the “App”). Your privacy — and your child’s privacy — is very important to us.

This Apple Store Policy explains how information is handled for the Apple App Store version of Pitter-Patter Raindrops.

1. Information We Collect

The Apple App Store version does not collect any personal information.

We do not collect, store, transmit, analyze, or share:

  • Names
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • IP addresses
  • Usage analytics, behavioral tracking, or telemetry

No user accounts, logins, or profiles are required to use the App.

2. Children’s Privacy

Pitter-Patter Raindrops is designed specifically for children.

We comply with:

  • The Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States
  • Applicable international children’s privacy laws

Because the App does not collect personal data, parental consent is not required for use.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise their child’s digital activity.

3. How the App Works

The App functions as a tap-to-play and read interactive storybook. All interactions occur locally on the device.

  • No information is transmitted to external servers
  • No online features, messaging, or social sharing exist
  • No login, registration, or sign-up is required

4. Third-Party Services

The Apple App Store version does not use third-party services that collect or track user data.

The App does not include:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Analytics tools
  • Tracking or profiling technologies
  • Social media integrations
  • External links directed at children

5. Purchases & Payments

Pitter-Patter Raindrops is a one-time paid purchase handled by the Apple App Store.

  • The App contains no in-app purchases.
  • We do not receive or store payment information.
  • Refunds are subject to the Apple App Store policies.

6. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal information for us to store on servers.

The App operates without accessing sensitive device permissions.

7. Audio & White Noise Playback

Pitter-Patter Raindrops includes an optional white noise audio feature designed to support calm rest after the story ends.

  • The App plays audio only and does not record, listen to, or monitor sound.
  • The App does not access the device microphone.
  • No audio data is collected, stored, transmitted, or shared.
  • All audio playback happens locally on the device.

8. Recommended Audio Use for Young Children (Ages 0–5)

  • Keep the device at least 6–10 feet away during sleep.
  • Use low volume.
  • Avoid placing the device directly in a crib or bed.

Welcome to Pitter-Patter Raindrops (the “App”). Your privacy — and your child’s privacy — is very important to us.

This Google Playstore Policy explains how information is handled for the Google Play version of Pitter-Patter Raindrops.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for accounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, videos, or voice recordings.

However, the Google Play version may use the following Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics (to understand general app usage and improve performance)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (to deliver optional app notifications, if enabled on the device)

These services may process limited information such as:

  • App interactions (for example: screens viewed, taps, feature usage) in aggregated form
  • Device and app information (for example: device model, OS version, app version, language, region)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (for example: country/region)
  • Identifiers used for app measurement and messaging delivery (for example: an app instance identifier and/or messaging token)

We do not use this data to show ads, build advertising profiles, or sell personal information.

2. Children’s Privacy

Pitter-Patter Raindrops is designed specifically for children.

We aim to comply with children’s privacy laws, including COPPA, and Google Play Families requirements where applicable.

We do not include third-party advertising, and we do not allow user-generated content, chat, or social features.

3. How the App Works

The App functions as a tap-to-play and read interactive storybook. Story interactions and most features run locally on the device.

  • No user accounts, registration, or sign-up is required
  • No social sharing, messaging, or external child-directed links exist inside the App

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase services (Analytics and Cloud Messaging). Firebase is provided by Google and may process data as described above in order to provide analytics measurement and notification delivery.

We use Firebase for:

  • App improvement (understanding what features are used, improving stability and user experience)
  • Optional notifications (for example: updates), only if notifications are enabled on the device

We do not use Firebase services for advertising, and we do not integrate advertising SDKs.

5. Push Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If notifications are enabled on your device, the App may receive a messaging token used to deliver notifications to that device.

  • Notifications are optional and can be turned off in device settings at any time.
  • The App does not read your messages, contacts, or other personal content.
  • We do not send notifications that request personal information from children.

6. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Firebase Analytics helps us understand overall app usage (for example, which screens are used most) and helps us improve performance and fix bugs.

  • Analytics data is used for product improvement, not for advertising.
  • We do not attempt to identify a child or parent from analytics data.

7. Purchases

Pitter-Patter Raindrops is a one-time paid purchase handled by Google Play Store. The App contains no in-app purchases, and we do not receive or store payment information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our services. Firebase data is processed on Google infrastructure with security measures designed to protect it.

9. Audio & White Noise Playback

The App includes optional white noise. The App does not access the microphone and does not record sound. Audio playback happens locally on the device.
